# Skeptical Review Skill

When using this skill:

1. Identify weak assumptions.
2. Look for hidden costs and second-order effects.
3. Ask what evidence is missing.
4. Identify failure modes.
5. Distinguish between:
   - fatal problems
   - manageable risks
   - unknowns that need testing
6. Do not be negative for its own sake.

Output style:
- Precise
- Calm
- Evidence-focused
- Constructively critical
